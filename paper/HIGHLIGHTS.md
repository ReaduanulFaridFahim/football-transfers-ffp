# Research Highlights (Mandatory for Journal Submission)

- **Machine Learning Tactical Void Modeling:** Ensemble CatBoost and LightGBM regressors predict post-departure team points-per-game ($\Delta\text{PPG}$) collapse with test $R^2 = 0.3485$ and $\text{MAE} = 0.1542$ across 1.89M match appearances and 176k manager fixtures.
- **Minutes Reliance Dominance:** Global SHAP feature attribution reveals that pitch minutes reliance share ($\rho_s = 0.184$) and baseline team quality ($\bar{P}_{\mathcal{C}} = 0.142$) govern sporting shock significantly more than gross market transfer valuation ($0.076$).
- **Regulatory Amortization & Arbitrage Gold Zone:** Integrates UEFA 70% Squad Cost Rule and PL PSR £105M caps with IAS 38 straight-line amortization, uncovering Pareto optimal recruitments that replicate $90\%+$ tactical production while capturing 40% to 75% fee arbitrage.
- **Macroeconomic Tournament Simulation:** A 10,000-run Monte Carlo tournament simulator shows that optimal replacement elevates Champions League qualification odds from 44.1% to 88.4%, safeguarding €73.7M in European prize money.
- **Validated Retrospective & Prospective Utility:** Validated on landmark departures (Harry Kane 2023, Jürgen Klopp 2024) and prospective 2026/27 European succession planning scenarios with an open-source reproducibility codebase.
